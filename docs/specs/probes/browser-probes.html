Game UI browser probes

Five checks the dm-game skills make about pointer lock, Esc and held keys. Run each in order, then press Copy results and paste them back. About two minutes per browser.

1 · Relock after Esc (cooldown)

  1. Click the box to lock the pointer.
  2. Press Esc to release it.
  3. Click Resume immediately, then click it again about 2 s later.
click to lock

2 · Programmatic exit, then relock without a click

  1. Click Start. It locks the pointer, then the page releases it after 1 s (like a game opening its menu).
  2. Don't click anything. 2 s later the page asks for the lock again from a timer, with no gesture.

3 · Does the page see the Esc that exits lock or fullscreen?

  1. Click Fullscreen, then press Esc once.
  2. Lock the pointer (box above), then press Esc once.

4 · Held Enter on a focused button

  1. Tab to (or click) Hold Enter here, then hold Enter for about 2 s and release.
  2. Do the same with Space.
